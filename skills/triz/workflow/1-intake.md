# Stage 1: Intake

> Draft for v0.1. Expanded in v0.3.

**Goal:** understand the problem well enough to state it back in one paragraph
and describe the ideal result.

## Steps

1. Ask the user to describe the problem in their own words.
2. Ask 3-5 clarifying questions, one at a time:
   - What is the goal? What does success look like?
   - What have you already tried, and what happened?
   - What are the constraints (time, money, people, tools)?
   - What must not change?
   - Who is affected?
3. Restate the problem in one paragraph.
4. Write the Ideal Final Result (IFR): "The system does X by itself, with no
   added cost or harm."
5. List the resources already present: things, people, time, information, and
   by-products or waste that could be reused.

## Output

Problem statement, IFR, resource list, constraint list. Update the state block.

## Gate

Ask the user to confirm the restatement and IFR. Do not continue until they do.
